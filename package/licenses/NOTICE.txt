reEye (PPSA99070): GoldenEye 007 (Xbox 360 / XBLA) statically recompiled for the PS5

An unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Rare, Microsoft, Nintendo, Activision, Danjaq, EON
Productions or Sony Interactive Entertainment. It contains none of the game's files.

What is in the app, and under which terms

- reEye's own code, the PS5 host (ps5/) and its changes to the SDK and the port: GPL-3.0 (reEye-GPL-3.0.txt). The
  PS5 entry and the POSIX replacements come from reKing (GPL-3.0), which follows reUndr's PS5 host by bystritskiy
  (MIT, reUndr-MIT.txt).
- GoldenEye-Recomp by SunJaycy, the PC port's hooks, fixes and configuration (The Unlicense,
  GoldenEye-Recomp-Unlicense.txt).
- The ReXGlue SDK (its GoldenEye fork), the recompiler and the Xbox 360 runtime, derived from Xenia (BSD-3-Clause,
  ReXGlue-BSD-3-Clause.txt), and the third-party code it builds in under their own licences.
- Dear ImGui (MIT), toml++ (MIT), the Khronos Vulkan headers (Apache-2.0 / MIT), glslang (BSD / MIT / Apache-2.0,
  glslang.txt).
- RADV from Mesa (MIT and others, Mesa-license.rst), built with PS5_Vulkan's recipe, which with its native link
  recipe and the PS5 Platform library from the PS5_PayloadSDK fork is GPL-3.0-or-later (PS5_Vulkan-LICENSE.txt,
  PS5_PayloadSDK-LICENSE.txt). The combined executable is therefore subject to the GPL-3.0-or-later.
